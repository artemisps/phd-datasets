# Social Innovation and City Planning: Applications for Urban Governance and Participatory Design

## Overview

This repository supports the doctoral research of Artemis Psaltoglou at the Department of Urban and Regional Planning, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. The dissertation investigates the transformative potential of digital applications in fostering social innovation and enhancing citizen empowerment within urban governance, particularly in urban planning and design contexts. It integrates multidisciplinary methods from urban studies, social sciences, computer science, and design theory.

## Research Focus

The core aim of this research is to explore how digital technologies can support more open, inclusive, and participatory processes in urban planning and design. The research specifically addresses:

- The integration of social innovation frameworks in digital participatory practices.
- The use of playful and game-based approaches for civic engagement.
- The mapping and evaluation of digital platforms for participatory planning.
- The development of interactive visualization tools using spatial and user-generated data.
- The formulation of design principles for digitally mediated public spaces.

## Research Questions

The study is guided by the central question:

**How can digital applications contribute to the shift towards a more open, participatory, and socially inclusive process of urban planning and design?**

This is further elaborated through sub-questions aligned with different dissertation chapters, addressing issues such as environmental sustainability, gamification, data visualization, and co-creation.

## Methodology

A mixed methods approach is adopted, combining:

- **Qualitative analysis**: Case studies, thematic analysis, role-playing workshops.
- **Quantitative analysis**: Descriptive statistics, regression, clustering.
- **Experimental research**: Development and testing of prototypes using spatial and user-generated data in game engines.

## Structure

The research is structured across eight chapters, each exploring a distinct thematic and methodological aspect:

1. **Introduction**
2. **Research Design**
3. **Navigating complexity: social innovation approaches to contemporary challenges**
4. **Gaming the process: Social Innovation and playful approaches**
5. **Mapping digital participation: a comparative analysis of digital applications for participatory planning and design**
6. **Rethinking visualization: Urban data for citizen empowerment in urban planning and design**
7. **Designing for all: inclusive and digitally mediated public spaces**
8. **Conclusions and Recommendations**

## Contributions

- A structured analytical framework bridging social innovation and urban planning.
- Empirical evidence from a global selection of social innovation and digital participation practices.
- Prototyping of novel tools for data visualization and participatory engagement.
- A set of actionable design principles for co-creating inclusive, digitally mediated public spaces.
